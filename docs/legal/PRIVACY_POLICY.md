By using Lynks, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this policy.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

### 3.1 Primary Uses

1. **Career Roadmap Generation** — Your profile data (name, age, education, interests, employment status) is processed by AI to generate personalised career roadmaps with specific tasks and milestones.

2. **Evidence Verification** — Uploaded evidence files (images, PDFs) are stored in cloud storage and may be analysed by AI to verify they represent genuine career achievements.

3. **Mentor Chat** — Your chat messages are processed by AI to provide career guidance. Conversations may be stored to maintain context across sessions.

4. **Opportunity Matching** — Your profile is used to match you with relevant scholarships, competitions, internships, and job opportunities.

5. **Notifications** — We send notifications about new opportunities, task milestones, and reminders related to your career roadmap.

### 3.2 Secondary Uses

- **Service Improvement** — Aggregated, anonymised usage data helps us improve the platform
- **Security** — Monitoring for unauthorised access and abuse
- **Legal Compliance** — Responding to lawful requests from authorities

## 6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Encryption in Transit** | All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS |
| **Encryption at Rest** | Database and storage data is encrypted at rest by our cloud providers |
| **Authentication** | Secure JWT-based authentication with Supabase Auth |
| **Row-Level Security** | Database policies ensure users can only access their own data |
| **Access Controls** | Backend security headers, rate limiting, and input validation |
| **Password Storage** | Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt via Supabase Auth |

### 6.1 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that may affect your personal information, we will:
1. Notify the **Personal Data Protection Board** (once established under the DPA 2011)
2. Notify affected users within **72 hours** of discovering the breach
3. Provide information about the nature of the breach and steps being taken
